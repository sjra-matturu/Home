Preserving Sanātana Dharma, Sanskrit Learning & Cultural Heritage

Sri Janaki Rama Ashrama was established on 04-09-2024 at Mattur, Shivamogga, by Śrī Śrī Bodhānandendra Sarasvatī Mahāsvāmigaḷu with the noble purpose of protecting, nurturing and propagating Veda-Vedānta, Saṁskṛta Bhāṣā, cultural and dharmic traditions.
