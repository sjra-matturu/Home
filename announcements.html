Institutional Objectives & Sacred Mission

Sri Janaki Rama Ashrama has been established for the preservation, propagation and revitalization of Vaidika, Sanskritic, cultural and dhārmic traditions for the welfare of society.

Objectives of the Institution

  1. To impart foundational and advanced teachings of the Veda, Upaniṣads, śāstras, Saṁskṛta literature, and traditional Indian knowledge systems.
  2. To convene sabhās and symposiums dedicated to the exposition and preservation of Veda - Vedānta, yajña - yāga, and the ancient Śrauta traditions enshrined within the eternal Vaidika heritage.
  3. To provide students and aspirants an opportunity to receive instruction directly from accomplished scholars and learned paṇḍitas.
  4. To preserve and propagate the profound treasures of Sanātana Dharma through Saṁskṛta learning.
  5. To facilitate the study of various śāstric disciplines while simultaneously encouraging learners to internalize and practice dhārmic values in daily life.
  6. To undertake the protection, nurturing, and preservation of indigenous cows and to establish and maintain goshalas dedicated to their care and welfare.
  7. To gather and mobilize the necessary resources, assistance, and support required for the advancement of the institution’s religious, educational, cultural, and charitable activities.
  8. To make the knowledge of Sanātana Dharma accessible to all sections of society.
  9. To provide suitable facilities and necessary assistance for differently abled persons.
  10. To organize programmes for the preservation and protection of traditional, cultural, and classical fine arts.
